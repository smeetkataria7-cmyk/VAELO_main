Sky Gem

ClientSky Gem
CategoryJewellery
ScopeAI Production + Social Media Marketing
Year2026

The brief

What was
in the way

Sky Gem sells fine diamond jewellery under one line: simple, pure, authentic. In a feed full of glossy product spins, that promise has to be felt in a few seconds, on the skin, in real light. Shooting every piece in a studio for every drop was slow and expensive.

What we did

The work
itself

We produced the reels with AI, building each one around the piece worn close to the skin so the stones catch the light the way they do in real life. The same warm, quiet look runs through every clip, so the feed reads as one brand. The reels were cut for vertical placement and published as part of the social media marketing.
