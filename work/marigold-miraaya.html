The brief

What was
in the way

Marigold Miraaya had reached the point most developments treat as the finish line \u2014 OC received, ready to move in, MahaRERA P51800003669. The category answers that moment with specification: carpet area, configuration, possession date. None of it tells a buyer what living there is actually like, and every competing listing reads the same way.

What we did

The work
itself

We shot the building the way residents use it and built the campaign around moments rather than amenities \u2014 carrom in the indoor games room, a couple in the poolside cabana, families in the water at the same hour. The line carries the argument: Evenings Well Spent. Unwind. Together. Something for Everyone. The specification and the RERA number stay on the pack where they belong; the campaign sells the evening.
