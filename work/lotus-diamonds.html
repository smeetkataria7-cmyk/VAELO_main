ClientLotus Diamonds
CategoryColored Diamonds
ScopeAI Production + Social Media Marketing
Year2026

The brief

What was
in the way

Diamond jewellery lives or dies on light. Every piece has to sparkle on a small phone screen in a feed full of the same close-up on black velvet. The brand needed a steady supply of premium moving content for social, without the cost and delay of a studio shoot for every collection.

What we did

The work
itself

We produced the films with AI, directing each one as a short scene built around the jewellery rather than a plain product shot. The same visual language runs through every clip, so the feed reads as one brand. The films were cut for vertical social placement and used as the core of the social media marketing.
