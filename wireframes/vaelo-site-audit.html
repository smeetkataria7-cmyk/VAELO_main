Blockers

Ranked by what it costs you today

Six things that lose you a visitor. Line numbers are in the file as it stands.

01

No email. No phone. No form.

The contact section says “Let's build something people can't ignore” — then offers your own domain and an Instagram handle. A ready client has nowhere to go.

Lost leadlines 191–198
02

The portfolio is five placeholders

Five stock picsum.photos images with invented titles, and every one links to #work — the section it already sits in. Clicking your work does nothing.

The product is missinglines 168–172
03

The wide work tile breaks the mobile grid

It spans 2 columns in a grid that becomes 1 column on phones, so CSS invents a second column and the page grows wider than the screen. You never see it because overflow-x:hidden on the body is silently swallowing it.

Broken on phonesline 172 + line 21
04

The hero can load as an empty black box

The background video has no poster image. iOS Low Power Mode or any autoplay refusal leaves flat black behind the headline — and the code catches that failure and ignores it. The clip is also hotlinked from a third-party CDN.

First impressionlines 146, 227
05

Mobile visitors can't navigate

The nav is set to display:none below 700px with no hamburger to replace it. Work, Services and Contact simply vanish — on the devices your Instagram traffic arrives on.

Dead endline 37
06

Shared links render as a grey nothing

No description, no Open Graph or Twitter card, no favicon. Every WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Slack share of vaelocreative.com shows a bare URL — no image, no title. For a visual company that's the most expensive omission on the page.

Two smaller piles

Accessibility

  • No focus styles anywhere — keyboard navigation is completely invisible
  • Headings jump h1 → h3; section labels are divs, so there's no document outline
  • All five work images carry empty alt text — your portfolio reads as decoration
  • No <main>, no landmarks, no labelled sections
  • Service rows respond to hover only — nothing for touch or keyboard
  • The reduced-motion CSS rule disables animations but not transitions
  • 11.5px label text sits below comfortable reading size

Developer experience — your actual complaint

  • vaelo-agency (1).html is a byte-identical duplicate. Two sources of truth. Delete it
  • Adding one case study means hand-editing a 130-character tag with five nested divs
  • No data layer at all — work, services and stats are hardcoded in markup
  • Four render-blocking files in the head, none deferred (~70KB of GSAP)
  • The font preconnect points at the wrong host, so it saves nothing
  • Inline style= overrides fight the stylesheet in four places

Order I'd do it in

Design direction stays untouched throughout

The look isn't the problem. This rebuilds the shell around it.

Fix the six blockers in place

Grid span, video poster, mobile nav, real contact details, focus states, working links. No redesign — the site just stops being broken.
